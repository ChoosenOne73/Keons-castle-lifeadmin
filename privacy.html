1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Keon's Castle LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information across LifeAdmin, Leftovers, Neighborhood Helper, Keon's Castle Auto Care, Roadside Warriors, and related digital products (collectively, the "Services").

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and phone number provided at signup or checkout
  • Payment information: processed directly by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers
  • Service-specific information: for example, documents or photos you choose to scan within LifeAdmin, pantry items within Leftovers, task or location details within Neighborhood Helper, vehicle and appointment details within Auto Care, or roadside request details within Roadside Warriors
  • Camera and photo access: certain features (such as document scanning or provider identity verification) request access to your device camera or photo library, used only for the specific feature requesting it and only with your permission
  • Communications: messages sent through in-app contact forms or support requests
  • Usage data: general information about how the Services are used, for the purpose of improving functionality

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to: provide and maintain the Services, process payments and subscriptions, communicate with you about your account or purchases, verify identity for marketplace provider accounts, improve and troubleshoot the Services, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Stripe, for payment processing
  • Supabase, for secure database hosting
  • Other users or providers as necessary to fulfill a requested service (for example, sharing your location with a Roadside Warriors provider you have accepted help from)
  • Law enforcement or regulators where required by law

5. Documents, Photos, and Scanned Content

Where a Service includes document or photo scanning (such as LifeAdmin or Leftovers), images may be processed on your own device to extract information such as dates or text, and relevant extracted data may be securely stored to provide the Service. You are responsible for the accuracy and sensitivity of documents you choose to scan or upload.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and service information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@keonscastlellc.com. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to request deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and secure storage practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic functionality and understand usage patterns. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to hello@keonscastlellc.com.
